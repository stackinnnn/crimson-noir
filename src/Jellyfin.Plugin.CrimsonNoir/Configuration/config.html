Card details

Titles, progress bars and badges on posters: always shown, or only while pointing at a card (on devices with a mouse). People can change this for themselves.

Home

A full-width slideshow of titles at the top of Home. Off leaves Jellyfin's own Home rows.

Features

A small × on Continue Watching cards takes a title off the row, with Undo.

Family & sign-in

Age limit for featured picks

The Home slideshow, feature tiles and Pick for me only suggest titles up to this rating (unrated titles are left out too). The libraries themselves aren't affected; use each account's parental controls for that.

Sign-in screen artwork

The artwork behind the sign-in screen, which anyone can see before signing in. Family-friendly shows U and PG titles only. Each device picks up a change the next time someone signs in on it.

Movies & Shows

Wide tiles between the rows of posters on Movies, Shows, Collections and Favourites, picked from what each person likes and hasn't seen.

A button on Movies and Shows that suggests a random title, with quick filters.

A badge on show posters when an episode the viewer hasn't seen aired recently.

Title pages

Film and episode pages show the credits, release and picture & sound panel straight away.

Box sets show the films you don't have on the shelf; people's pages show their best-known work outside the library. Uses Seerr through Jellyfin Enhanced when installed (and offers Request), otherwise the TMDB key below.

Only needed without Jellyfin Enhanced and Seerr. A free v3 API key or v4 read access token from themoviedb.org > Settings > API. It stays on the server.

Profile menu

The other accounts appear at the bottom of the profile menu; choosing one signs out and opens it on the sign-in screen. Turn off if accounts shouldn't see each other.

Top bar

Drag the links into order (or use the arrows) and switch off anything you don't want. Home always comes first; Back and the profile button always stay. Discover, Requests and Calendar only appear when Jellyfin Enhanced provides them.
